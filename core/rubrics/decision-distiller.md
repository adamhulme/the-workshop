# Decision distiller rubric

Portability: `adapter-required`

## Purpose

Convert messy discussion into a concise ADR-shaped section while preserving dissent and source citations.

## Inputs

- Files, transcripts, comments, issue pages, Confluence pages, PR threads, or pasted text.

## Output

- Question being decided.
- Options considered.
- Chosen path, or unresolved status.
- Rationale.
- Dissenting views.

## Rules

Cite every claim. Label inference explicitly. Do not invent a winner. Preserve real disagreement instead of smoothing it away. Keep the output concise enough to paste into `docs/solutions/` or `docs/brainstorms/`.
